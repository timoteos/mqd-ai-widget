# How do I find or choose a doctor?

Your **primary care provider (PCP)** sees you for checkups and when you're sick, and arranges specialists.

**Find a doctor** in your health plan's directory:
- [AlohaCare](https://www.alohacare.org/QUEST/MemberDocuments.aspx)
- [HMSA](https://hmsa.com/search/providers/)
- [Kaiser Permanente](http://kpquest.org/benefits)
- 'Ohana Health Plan and UnitedHealthcare Community Plan: see the [provider directory page](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/already-covered/Health-Plans/Provider-Directory) or call your plan.

**Choosing:** your plan's welcome packet has a form to choose a PCP. You have **15 days** from your enrollment start date; if you don't choose, the plan assigns one. You can **change your PCP at any time** by contacting your plan.

Have a Medicare Advantage plan with a PCP already? Tell your health plan the name of that doctor.

More: [Start Your New Health Plan](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/your-new-plan)
