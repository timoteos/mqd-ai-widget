# Can Med-QUEST give me a ride to my medical appointment?

Yes. **Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)** is a free ride to and from medically necessary appointments when you have no other way to get there. It is **not** for emergencies: call 911.

**You may qualify if** you're a QUEST Integration health plan member, the appointment is for a covered service, you have no other transportation, and your doctor or health coordinator confirms the ride is medically necessary.

**Book through your health plan, at least 48 hours ahead:**
- AlohaCare: 1-877-973-0712 or [book online](https://transdevhealthsolutions.com/alohacare/)
- HMSA: 1-800-440-0640 or [Modivcare](https://www.modivcare.com/facilities/hmsa/)
- Kaiser Permanente: 1-855-735-1226
- 'Ohana Health Plan: 1-888-846-4262
- UnitedHealthcare Community Plan: 1-888-777-3844

Rides can be a bus pass, curb-to-curb or door-to-door service, or air travel to another island or out of state for care. Urgent visits (within 24 hours) can be booked with less notice, and regular rides (like dialysis) can be scheduled for the whole quarter. There is no cost to you.

Ride denied? Contact your health plan, or the Medicaid Ombudsman at 888-488-7988.
