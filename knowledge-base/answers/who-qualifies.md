# Who can get Med-QUEST?

Med-QUEST covers Hawai'i residents who qualify in groups including:

- **Children** under 19 (and some young people up to 26 who were in foster care)
- **Pregnant women**
- **Parents and caretaker relatives** living with a child under 19
- **Adults** 19 to 64
- **People who are aged (65+), blind or disabled**

**Income:** most people qualify based on household income, up to these levels of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL): children up to 313%, pregnant women up to 196%, parents and caretakers up to 105%, and adults under 65 up to 138%.

**65 and over, blind or disabled:** income up to 100% FPL and assets up to $2,000 for one person ($3,000 for two). People above that may still qualify through the Medically Needy Spenddown program if their medical bills are high.

**The only way to know for sure is to apply,** and it's free: [apply online](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov) or see [all the ways to apply](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/how-to-apply).
