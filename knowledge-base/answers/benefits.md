# What does Med-QUEST cover?

QUEST Integration health plans cover, among other things:

- **Doctor visits,** preventive care, immunizations and urgent care
- **Hospital care,** including maternity and newborn care, and emergency services with ambulance
- **Prescription drugs**
- **Vision and hearing services,** and medical equipment including visual appliances
- **Behavioral health:** mental health care, psychiatric care, and alcohol and drug treatment
- **Pregnancy-related services** and family planning
- **Lab tests, imaging, dialysis,** rehabilitation, home health and hospice
- **Rides to appointments** (see "Can Med-QUEST give me a ride?")
- **Long-term services and supports** for people who qualify

Med-QUEST also covers **dental care**, arranged separately from your health plan: you find a dentist through Community Case Management Corp, O'ahu 808-792-1070 or toll-free 1-888-792-1070 (see the [Dental page](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/quest-integration-coverage/Dental)).

For details of what your plan covers and whether something needs approval first, check your health plan's member handbook or call the number on your member card.
