# I have Medicare and Medicaid. Can I join a dual special needs plan?

Yes. People with both Medicare and Medicaid can join a dual eligible special needs plan (D-SNP).

**Since January 1, 2026:** if you're newly enrolling, you can only join the **FIDE SNP** offered by the same health plan company as your QUEST Integration health plan. HIDE SNPs no longer take new members.

**Already in a HIDE SNP?** If you were enrolled on December 31, 2025, you can stay in your HIDE SNP and your QUEST Integration plan.

The [Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan page](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/Dual-Eligible-Special-Needs-Plan) compares the plans and lists each plan's contact number.
