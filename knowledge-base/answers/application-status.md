# How do I check the status of my application?

I can't see individual applications. To check on yours:

- **If you applied online,** sign in to your account at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).
- **Or call** the Customer Services Call Center at 1-800-316-8005 (O'ahu 808-524-3370, TTY 711). They can check your application status and tell you your worker's name and phone number.

If Med-QUEST needs more information to decide, they will contact you, so answer any letter right away.
