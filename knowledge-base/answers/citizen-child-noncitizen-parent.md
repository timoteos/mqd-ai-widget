# I'm not a U.S. citizen but my baby was born here. Can my baby get Med-QUEST?

**Yes, your baby can get Med-QUEST if your household income qualifies.** A baby born in the United States is a U.S. citizen, so your baby's citizenship counts, not yours.

- **You can apply for your baby only.** You don't have to apply for yourself.
- **If you are not applying for yourself, you don't have to give your own immigration status or Social Security number.** You will still be asked about your household and income, because that decides whether your baby qualifies.
- **If you had Med-QUEST coverage when your baby was born,** your baby may already be covered. Report the birth so your baby is added.

How to apply for your baby:

- Online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov)
- On paper with [Form DHS 1100](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20%28Rev.%2004.%202023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINALje06.07.24.pdf)
- By phone at 1-800-316-8005

If you have questions about how applying could affect an immigration case, an immigration attorney or legal aid office can advise you.
