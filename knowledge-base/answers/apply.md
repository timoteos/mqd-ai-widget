# How do I apply for Med-QUEST?

There are three ways to apply. Applying online is the fastest.

**1. Online (fastest):** apply at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).

**2. On paper:** fill in [Form DHS 1100, Application for Health Coverage and Help Paying Costs](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20%28Rev.%2004.%202023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINALje06.07.24.pdf) ([instructions](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20Instructions%20%28Rev.%2004.2023%29FINAL.pdf)). Print it and mail or fax it to the [eligibility office nearest you](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us). The fax number is 1-800-576-5504.

**3. By phone:** call 1-800-316-8005 (O'ahu 808-524-3370). TTY users call 711.

Fill in the application as completely as you can; complete applications are decided faster. If Med-QUEST needs more information, they will contact you.

Applying with a disability, or for long-term care? You need extra forms with the application (see "What extra forms do I need?").

More: [Apply for Medicaid](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/how-to-apply)
