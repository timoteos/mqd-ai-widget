# Who is the Med-QUEST director?

**Meredith Nichols, M.A.**, is the Med-QUEST Administrator and Medicaid Director for the State of Hawaii Department of Human Services. She was previously Deputy Medicaid Director and Assistant Administrator for Med-QUEST from 2017 to 2026.

Med-QUEST is a division of the Department of Human Services and runs Hawaii's Medicaid program.

For help with your own coverage or application, it's quickest to start online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov), or call Med-QUEST Customer Service at 1-800-316-8005 (TTY 711).
