# Where can I find Med-QUEST forms?

All member forms are on the [Member Forms page](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/member-forms). The most used:

- **Apply:** [DHS 1100, Application for Health Coverage](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20%28Rev.%2004.%202023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINALje06.07.24.pdf) ([instructions](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20Instructions%20%28Rev.%2004.2023%29FINAL.pdf))
- **Report a change:** [DHS 1179A, Change of Circumstance Report](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201179A%20%28Rev.%2002.2023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINAL.pdf) ([instructions](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201179A%20Instructions%20%28Rev.%2002.2023%29%20FINAL.pdf))
- **Age, blindness, disability or long-term care:** [DHS 1100B](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/EB_Fillable_DHS_1100B_Rev_04-2021_JL10-28-22.pdf)

Many things don't need a form at all: you can apply, renew and report changes online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).

Mail or fax completed forms to your [eligibility office](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us) (fax 1-800-576-5504).
