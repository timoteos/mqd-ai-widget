# How do I report a change, like a new address, income or household?

Tell Med-QUEST when you move, change your name, get or change a job, your income or assets change, or someone joins or leaves your household (a birth, death, marriage or divorce).

**1. Online (fastest):** sign in to your account at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).

**2. On paper:** fill in [Form DHS 1179A, Change of Circumstance Report](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201179A%20%28Rev.%2002.2023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINAL.pdf) ([instructions](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201179A%20Instructions%20%28Rev.%2002.2023%29%20FINAL.pdf)) and send it to the [eligibility office on your island](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us) by mail or fax (1-800-576-5504).

**3. By phone:** call 1-800-316-8005 (TTY 711).

Keep your address and phone number current: Med-QUEST sends letters with deadlines, and missing one is a common reason people lose coverage. For a change of address on your health plan member card, also tell your health plan.
