# I lost my card. How do I get a new one?

You may have two cards:

- **Health plan member card** (from AlohaCare, HMSA, Kaiser, 'Ohana or UnitedHealthcare): call your health plan.
  - AlohaCare 1-877-973-0712
  - HMSA 1-800-440-0640
  - Kaiser Permanente 1-800-651-2237
  - 'Ohana Health Plan 1-888-846-4262
  - UnitedHealthcare Community Plan 1-888-980-8728
- **Medicaid ID card:** call Med-QUEST at 1-800-316-8005 (O'ahu 808-524-3370, TTY 711).

More: [Contact Us](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us)
