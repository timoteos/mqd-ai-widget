# I can't log in to my online account. What do I do?

Try these first:

- Use a supported browser on a computer: **Chrome 92+, Edge 92+ or Firefox 90+.** Tablets and smartphones are not compatible with the portal.
- Clear your browser history and cache, then try again.
- To reset your password, watch the [password reset video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKW19fybY4s).

Still stuck? Contact the **login help desk:** 1-808-900-7979, Monday to Friday, 7:45 am to 4:30 pm, or email [MQDLoginHelpDesk@dhsie.com](mailto:MQDLoginHelpDesk@dhsie.com).
