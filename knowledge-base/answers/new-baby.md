# I had a baby. Is my baby covered?

If you were enrolled in a QUEST Integration health plan on the day your baby was born, your **newborn is automatically enrolled in your health plan for at least 30 days** from birth. Med-QUEST will mail you an enrollment packet, and you can change the baby's health plan after the first 30 days.

Report the birth to Med-QUEST so your household information is up to date: online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov), with [Form DHS 1179A](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201179A%20%28Rev.%2002.2023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINAL.pdf), or by calling 1-800-316-8005.
