# What extra forms do I need to apply if I have a disability or need long-term care?

Send these with your application, [Form DHS 1100](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS%201100%20%28Rev.%2004.%202023%29%20Fillable%20Form%20FINALje06.07.24.pdf):

**If you have a disability:**
- [DHS 1100B, Supplemental Form for Age, Blindness, Disability, or Long-Term Care](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/EB_Fillable_DHS_1100B_Rev_04-2021_JL10-28-22.pdf)
- [DHS 1127, Medical History and Disability Statement](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_1127_Form_Rev_10_2022.pdf)
- [DHS 1128, Disability Report Form](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_1128_Form_Rev_10_2022.pdf)

**If you need long-term care services:**
- [DHS 1100B](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/EB_Fillable_DHS_1100B_Rev_04-2021_JL10-28-22.pdf)
- [DHS 1167, Statement of Intent to Return Home](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_1167_Form_Rev_12_16_Fillable.pdf)
- [DHS 1169, Evaluation for the Placement of Liens](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_1169_Rev_11_2020_FILLABLE.pdf)
- [DHS 1169A, Maintenance of Lien Status](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_1169A_Rev_11_2020_FILLABLE.pdf)
- [DHS 8003, Reporting Requirements for Long-Term Care with Annuities](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS8003-form-Rev1016.pdf)
- [DHS 8004, Annual Home Equity Survey](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/api/media/file/DHS_8004_Rev_06_17.pdf)

Long-term care also needs a level-of-care assessment, which you arrange with your doctor.
