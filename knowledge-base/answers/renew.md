# How do I renew my Med-QUEST coverage?

Med-QUEST reviews your information every year. When it's your turn, you'll get a **pink letter** in the mail, a text, an email, or all three. Some people are renewed automatically and just get a letter confirming it.

Three ways to renew:

**1. Online (fastest):** at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov), even if you don't have an online account yet.

**2. On paper:** fill in the form that came with your pink renewal notice and mail or fax it to your [eligibility office](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us).

**3. By phone:** call 1-800-316-8005 (TTY 711). If they need documents, you'll send them by mail, email or the online portal.

If you don't renew by the due date, your coverage could stop. You can still reapply at any time.
