# What are the new work requirements starting in 2027?

A federal law (H.R.1) changes the rules for some adults **starting January 1, 2027**.

**Who:** adults aged 19 to 64 who are not blind, disabled or pregnant. Some people don't have to meet the rule, including people with a disability, parents of children under 14, and people who are pregnant.

**What changes:**
- **Renewals every 6 months** instead of once a year.
- **A work requirement:** each month you'll need to work or volunteer at least **80 hours**, or earn at least **$580**, or be enrolled at least half-time in school. You'll need to show proof.

**How to get ready now:**
1. Set up your online account at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).
2. Make sure Med-QUEST has your current address and phone number; update them online or call 1-800-316-8005.
3. Answer any letter from Med-QUEST right away. Missing a deadline is a common reason people lose coverage.
