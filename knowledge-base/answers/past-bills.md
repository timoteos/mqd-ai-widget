# Can Med-QUEST pay medical bills from before I applied?

Possibly. This is called **retroactive coverage**, and the period is getting shorter:

- **If you apply by December 31, 2026:** Med-QUEST will cover approved costs for up to **3 months** before you applied.
- **From January 1, 2027:** up to **1 month** before you apply for most adults 19 to 64 without a disability, and up to **2 months** for children, adults 65 and older, and people with a disability.

If you got care before your enrollment notice arrived, tell your doctor or provider; your health plan may cover some of it.

More: [Stay Well, Stay Covered](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/resources/Stay_Well_Stay_Covered_Toolkit)
