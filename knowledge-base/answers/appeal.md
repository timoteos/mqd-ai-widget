# How do I appeal a Med-QUEST decision?

**If you disagree with an eligibility decision** (for example a denial, or your coverage ending), you can ask for:
- an **informal review** with your eligibility worker or their supervisor, and/or
- an **administrative hearing** with a hearing officer. You don't need an informal review first.

Ask in person, by phone (1-800-316-8005), by mail, or electronically. A written request can be on Med-QUEST's form or any paper. **Your request must reach Med-QUEST within 90 calendar days of the date on your notice.** Someone else can ask or speak for you.

**If your issue is with your health plan** (a denied service or a complaint), use your plan's grievance and appeal process, described in your member handbook and on the [Grievance and Appeals page](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/rights-and-responsibilities/grievance-appeals). The Medicaid Ombudsman helps with problems with a health plan: 1-888-488-7988, [Medicaid Ombudsman](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/resources/medicaid-ombudsman).
