# Which health plans can I choose, and how do I change my plan?

There are five QUEST Integration health plans:

| Health plan | Phone | Website |
|---|---|---|
| AlohaCare | 1-877-973-0712 | [alohacare.org](https://www.alohacare.org) |
| HMSA | 1-800-440-0640 | [hmsa.com/health-plans/quest](https://hmsa.com/health-plans/quest/) |
| Kaiser Permanente | 1-800-651-2237 | [kpquest.org](https://kpquest.org/) |
| 'Ohana Health Plan | 1-888-846-4262 | [ohanahealthplan.com](https://www.ohanahealthplan.com) |
| UnitedHealthcare Community Plan | 1-888-980-8728 | [uhccommunityplan.com/hawaii-medicaid-plans](https://www.uhccommunityplan.com/hawaii-medicaid-plans) |

Availability depends on enrollment limits.

**Choosing:** when you become eligible you get an enrollment choice packet. If you don't choose, Med-QUEST assigns a plan, and if you're happy with it you don't need to do anything.

**Changing:** you can switch
- within **15 days** of the date on your enrollment choice notice,
- at any time once you've been in your plan for **12 months**, or
- during the **annual plan change** period (you'll get a packet with a form).

To switch, tell Med-QUEST Customer Service: 1-800-316-8005 (O'ahu 808-524-3370, TTY 711). The new plan starts on the **first day of the following month**.

More: [Choose a Health Plan](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/health-plans)
