# My Med-QUEST coverage ended. What do I do?

It depends on why it ended:

**You forgot to renew, or Med-QUEST needed more information:**
- **Less than 90 days** since your renewal date: you can still complete your renewal and get your coverage reinstated, online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov).
- **More than 90 days:** [apply again](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/how-to-apply).

**You think it was a mistake:** you can appeal. See "How do I appeal a decision?" or talk to someone at a [Med-QUEST office](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us).

**You no longer qualify:** you can apply for coverage through the ACA marketplace at [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov).
