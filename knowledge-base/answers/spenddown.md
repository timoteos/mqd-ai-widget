# What is the Medically Needy Spenddown program?

It can help people whose **income** is over the limit but who have high medical bills. You may qualify if your medical expenses are more than a set amount (the "spenddown" amount).

**Who it's for:** children under 19, pregnant women, and people who are aged (65 or older), blind or disabled.

**For people who are aged, blind or disabled:** if income is over 100% of the Federal Poverty Level, you may still qualify through Spenddown, as long as your savings and other assets are within the limit: up to $2,000 for one person or $3,000 for two. Spenddown is about income; it doesn't help when savings are over the asset limit.

**To find out,** apply. It's free: [apply online](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov) or see [all the ways to apply](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/members-applicants/get-started/how-to-apply).
