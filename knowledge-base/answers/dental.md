# Does Med-QUEST cover dental care, and how do I find a dentist?

Yes.

**Under 21:** checkups and cleanings (diagnostic and preventive care) **once every six months**, plus endodontic therapy (root canals), oral surgery, periodontic therapy, prosthodontics and restorations (fillings).

**21 and older:** preventive, diagnostic and X-ray services, endodontic therapy, restorations, oral surgery, periodontal therapy, prosthodontics, and emergency and palliative treatment.

**Find a dentist who accepts Medicaid:** call Community Case Management Corp (CCMC): O'ahu 808-792-1070, neighbor islands 1-888-792-1070 (toll-free).
