# Can non-citizens get Med-QUEST?

You may be able to get or keep Med-QUEST coverage if:

- You are a **lawful permanent resident** (green card). Most green card holders must also wait 5 years.
- You are a **child up to age 19** lawfully residing in the United States.
- You are **pregnant or gave birth in the last 12 months** and are lawfully residing in the United States.
- You have **Cuban or Haitian entrant** status.
- You are a **citizen of Micronesia, the Marshall Islands or Palau** living in the United States.

If your situation isn't listed, your coverage may be affected by rules that began October 1, 2026. Apply or call 1-800-316-8005 to find out where you stand.
