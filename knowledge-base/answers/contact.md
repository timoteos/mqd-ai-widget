# How do I contact Med-QUEST?

Many things can be done online at [medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov](https://medical.mybenefits.hawaii.gov): applying, renewing and reporting changes.

**Phone:** 1-800-316-8005 (toll-free), O'ahu 808-524-3370. TTY users call 711.
**Fax:** 1-800-576-5504
**Email:** [MQDCustomerSupport@dhs.hawaii.gov](mailto:MQDCustomerSupport@dhs.hawaii.gov)

**Eligibility offices:**
- Honolulu: 1350 S. King Street, Suite 200
- Kapolei: 601 Kamokila Boulevard, Room 415
- Waipahu: 94-275 Mokuola Street, Suite 301
- Hilo (East Hawai'i): 1404 Kilauea Avenue
- Kailua-Kona (West Hawai'i): 75-5591 Palani Road, Suite 3004
- Maui: 210 Imi Kala Street, Suite 101, Wailuku
- Lāna'i: 730 Lanai Avenue, Lanai City
- Moloka'i: 65 Makaena Street, Room 110, Kaunakakai
- Kaua'i: 4473 Pahee Street, Suite A, Lihue

Call when you need someone to look at your own case: your application status, your worker's name, or a question about a letter you received.

More: [Contact Us](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/en/contact-us)
